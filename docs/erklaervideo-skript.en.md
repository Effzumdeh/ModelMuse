## Voiceover (continuous)

### 1. Hook — approx. 15 s

Hello and welcome to ModelMuse! Imagine typing in a lesson topic — and seconds later holding it in your hands as a rotatable 3D model, complete with labels and dimensions. Sounds like magic? Then watch closely, I'll show you everything step by step!

### 2. What is ModelMuse? — approx. 30 s

ModelMuse is your lab for vivid teaching — right in the browser. Nothing to install, no server needed: one HTML file runs on every classroom PC and tablet. Whether 3D assembly or 2D diagram — you rotate, zoom and explain everything live. Navigation is child's play: drag to orbit the model, scroll to zoom into details. And ModelMuse itself was built with AI — for a hackathon, by one person, for under five dollars.

### 3. Discovering models — approx. 30 s

To get you started, ten curated models await you: five in 3D — for example graphics card, hand drill, caffeine molecule and Brandenburg Gate — and five in 2D, such as circuit diagram, flowchart, bar chart, network plan and formula view. Choose a model from the library, rotate it with the mouse, zoom into details and read labels and dimensions right on the part. With the level switch you fade between Basic, Intermediate and Pro — so every explanation matches your class's level of knowledge.

### 4. Generating your own models — approx. 45 s

And now the exciting part: invent your own model! Just type a term into the free mode below — for example wind turbine. At the top choose the provider — Meta Muse or Baseten — with Muse Spark 1.3 as default. Enter your own API key once, and tick the box if it should be remembered for this session; it is stored nowhere else, never in the project. Click "Generate model", and ModelMuse builds your idea right in the browser and checks it automatically — counting labels, dimensions and tasks, so you instantly see what you got.

### 5. Editing, deleting, components — approx. 30 s

Something off? Describe your change in the Revise field — say red housing, twice as large — and the model reloads revised as a new session version. Delete what you no longer need from your session library with one click, or export it as a file first; the curated ten-model library stays untouched as reference. The components overlay toggles labels and dimensions individually — and 2D mode switches to the flat diagram view anytime.

### 6. Conclusion — approx. 15 s

ModelMuse: one file, ten models to start with and infinitely many ideas of your own — built with AI, made for your teaching. Try it right away: your next topic is already waiting. Have fun exploring!
