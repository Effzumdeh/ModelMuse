## Voiceover (continuous)

### 1. Hook — approx. 15 s

Never opened a 3D program? Perfect — ModelMuse is your lab, no CAD knowledge needed! Type in a topic — seconds later you can display it as a 3D model with labels and dimensions and have each component explained. I'll now show you step by step how to use this app. In just under three minutes you'll learn the tricks of using it.

### 2. What is ModelMuse? — approx. 30 s

ModelMuse runs on any PC and tablet, regardless of operating system and hardware: you only need a valid key, because the models work in the cloud. You rotate, zoom and explain live right on the model. ModelMuse is my solo entry for the Indie Developer Track of the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. It was built using only five dollars in credits, showing what is already possible with AI today.

### 3. What can you do with ModelMuse? — approx. 25 s

You get almost touchable 3D models. With the mouse you rotate them, with the scroll wheel you zoom in — from any angle, as close as you like. You can keep refining them until they fit your exact use case. The overlay fades labels, dimensions and components in right on the model — or out, depending on how you want to look at it.

### 4. Generating your own models — approx. 50 s

Here's how you create your model: simply enter a term in the generator — say wind turbine. Then choose the provider: Meta's own Muse or Baseten. Next pick the model: Spark for simple models, Wonder when it gets complicated or you want to revise in a targeted way. You enter your key once per session; it stays local with you. The reasoning levels let you fine-tune: Standard starts you off cheap, while xhigh or max make models especially detailed and true to measure — but also more expensive. A queue works through everything one by one, and you can submit new jobs in parallel while one is still running. Each finished model lands in your local session library and is checked automatically first — and if you ever hit an error message, you can file it as an issue on GitHub. That helps me keep improving the tool.

### 5. Editing, versions, export — approx. 35 s

Something off about your 3D model? Click the pen icon and describe your change requests: adding humans as a reference works just like changing colors, detailing components or resizing. That instantly creates a new version of your model. The history keeps ten versions per model, so you can keep iterating or return to earlier revisions. For worksheets and slides you export the view as PNG; as a GLB file the model runs in Blender, in Windows 3D Viewer and even in 3D print.

### 6. Outlook — approx. 20 s

And this is just the beginning: soon you'll polish models directly in the in-app editor, upload your own images as modelling base and bring your two-dimensional sketches or objects vividly to life in 3D. Missing your dream feature? Submit your feature idea via GitHub and help shape ModelMuse.

### 7. Conclusion — approx. 15 s

ModelMuse. Right in the browser. With current models from Meta. Zero prior knowledge needed. Built with AI for your use case.
